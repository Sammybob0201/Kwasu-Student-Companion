UNDERSTAND YOUR RESULT

How does your GPA work?

Your GPA is based on the credit units of your courses and the grade points you earn in each course.

01

Grade Point × Unit

Multiply the grade point earned in a course by the credit units of that course.

02

Add Quality Points

Add the quality points from all the courses you included in the semester.

03

Divide by Total Units

Divide your total quality points by your total credit units to get your GPA.

KWASU COMMUNITY

The People Behind KWASU.

Discover the people whose leadership, ideas, service and contributions have helped shape Kwara State University and its community.

FOUNDER & VISIONARY

Dr. Abubakar Bukola Saraki

Dr. Abubakar Bukola Saraki is a distinguished Nigerian medical doctor, former Governor, and 13th Senate President.As Governor, he served as the Visionary Founder of Kwara State University, signing its establishment bill into law in 2008.

FOUNDER & VISIONARY

Yusuf Umar Danshitta

Yusuf Umar Danshitta is an academic, researcher, and prominent institutional leader heavily involved with Kwara State University (KWASU). He represents the rising generation of student leaders who have transitioned into vital administrative and governance roles within their alma mater.

FOUNDER & VISIONARY

Professor AbdulRasheed Na'Allah

Abdul Rasheed Na'Allah is a Nigerian academic, educator and professor who served as the 6th Vice chancellor of University of Abuja from 2019 until June 2024. Prior to this, he was the Vice chancellor of Kwara State University from 2009 to 2019.

UNIVERSITY LEADERSHIP

Sheikh AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq

Dr. AbdulRazaq AbdulRaheem provides academic leadership as the Head of Department for Statistics and Mathematical Sciences at KWASU

UNIVERSITY LEADERSHIP

Dr.Kikelomo Wasilah Sallee

Dr. Kikelomo Wasilah Sallee serves as the official Registrar, Secretary to the University Senate, and Chief Administrative Officer of KWASU.She directly authorizes and signs all high-level academic, directorial, and executive personnel appointments across the university.

UNIVERSITY LEADERSHIP

Professor Shehu Usman Adam

Professor Shehu Usman Adam serves as the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic) of Kwara State University, steering the institution’s core learning policies.He previously provided pioneering leadership as the first Acting Dean of the newly formed Faculty of Management and Social Sciences.

ACADEMIC CONTRIBUTOR

Professor Abdulsalam Abiodun Salman

Professor Abdulsalam Abiodun Salman serves as the official University Librarian and a vital member of KWASU's principal executive management team.He spearheaded the successful listing of the Kwara State University Institutional Repository (KWASU IR).

ACADEMIC CONTRIBUTOR

Professor Gbolagade Kazeem

Professor Abdulsalam Abiodun Salman serves as the official University Librarian and a vital member of KWASU's principal executive management team.He spearheaded the successful listing of the Kwara State University Institutional Repository (KWASU IR) on the African Digital Research Repositories.

ACADEMIC CONTRIBUTOR

Dr. Bolatito Eunice

Dr. Bolatito Eunice Olanipekun serves as an expert lecturer and high-impact scholar within the Department of Chemistry at KWASU.

KWASU Alumni

Maryam Apaokagi(Taooma)

Maryam Apaokagi (Taaooma) is an esteemed Kwara State University alumna, a popular award-winning comedian and filmmaker who graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Tourism and Hospitality Management.

KWASU Alumni

Saheed Balogun
